01 / GET YOUR HANDS DIRTY

Manual, physical, unpredictable.

The current design centres on handling objects yourself. The earlier conveyor-and-crate experiment was retired as the game moved toward a more hands-on workshop.

Placeable props

FANS · PLATES · LIGHTS

Pick up the actual object, rotate it on selectable axes, then use a bottom-anchored ghost to place it against a solid surface. Attach props to other props. Upgraded fans and plates also lock in the exact held pose in mid-air. Switch fans and lights on or off with E.

Carry the chaos

BASKET · PUSH BROOM

A basket carries items that are physically inside it and empties through its bottom. The ground-following broom pushes groups of loose objects. Held objects fade by 35%, and the full carried assembly has its solid collisions disabled.

Things to grind

ART · WEIGHT · SOUND

The random pool includes the original test object, beer bottle, book, syringe and teddy bear. Imported textures, different masses, compact collisions, larger aim targets and material-specific impact sounds give each item its own behaviour. Chute cleanup prevents abandoned items building up.

Combo chains

1.0× → 1.5× → 2.0×

The first item starts a chain at 1.0×. Each following item adds 0.5× and refills a shorter countdown. The text-only HUD shows the chain; when it ends, the reward joins an accelerating, one-point-at-a-time score queue with matching sounds.

02 / SOMETHING IS WATCHING

A room. A door. A world beyond.

The environment reveals itself in stages, with saved flags controlling light, sound and interaction.

01 / WAKEStart on the floor.

Fade in from black, rise through body poses, slowly look around and regain control. Breathing, grunts, slide sounds and voiced dialogue support the sequence. Wall tally marks record starts and deaths.

02 / LEAVEKeep the door.

Interact to open it. After you exit, the concrete room is removed only when it is no longer visible; the Strange Door remains and closes automatically. Room footsteps bypass the outside sound gate.

03 / DISCOVERListen carefully.

The chute lights activate after departure, the grinder after five lever pulls, and the computer at 30 score. Bottle notes are unique drops with a reader and journal. Word-at-a-time messages and reusable voiced dialogue carry the story.

A doorway elsewhere

PORTAL PROTOTYPE

The retained door can reveal a remote test room at the same floor height, 1,600 metres away. Its perspective view, return route, held baskets and loose-item transfer are working. Colour, resolution and MSAA are matched between the view and the destination.

Death and return

GRINDER RESPAWN

Entering the grinder fades to black, plays a death voice and returns you to the concrete room. Progress and world items remain. One-time warnings and first-spawn or respawn dialogue are tracked separately.

03 / EARN MORE POSSIBILITIES

Two economies. One old computer.

Resource-driven purchases and upgrades keep prices, effects, dialogue and content editable without rebuilding the menus.

The terminal

MECHANICS · SELF · STORE

A retro interface sells props from the sky and upgrades future fans, plates and item value. Self upgrades add speed, jumping and multi-grab. Automatic spawners and interval upgrades extend production. Costs rise by tier and selected props grow more expensive with each purchase.

The Mulcher

GRIND · REPAIR · BANK

Buying the Mulcher mounts it automatically to the grinder. Items contribute mulch value and wear; every 100 mulch points produces a physical pellet. Deposit pellets in the Mulch Bin to bank permanent currency. Repair damaged machinery for escalating score, or upgrade its durability and efficiency.

Permanent upgrades

THE MULCH TAB

Owning the Mulcher and bin unlocks a fourth computer tab. Banked mulch purchases powerful bonuses that stack with ordinary upgrades and survive New Game, alongside the remaining mulch currency.

Save and return

TITLE · OPTIONS · BLACKBOARD

The title offers New Game, Continue when a saved run exists, Options and Exit. One file stores Blackboard, flags and the persistent profile; a loader restores the world before play. Shared Options controls Master, SFX, Vocals, Music, mouse sensitivity and axis inversion. Delete Save and Exit prevents shutdown from recreating the save.

04 / A WORKFLOW YOU CAN REUSE

See it. Report it. Record it.

The developer toolkit lives in one portable addon. Other Godot projects can replace its host adapter and keep the tools.

Local issue reports

F1 captures the game view before opening a description box. Local reports pair text with a screenshot. Repository instructions require future agents to read pending reports, actually view their images, implement the fix and record verification.

Inspect and command

F2 shows Blackboard, F3 flags and F4 the actual save file. The tilde console supports typed setters, score, teleport, fly and noclip. Commands are editable resources backed by scripts, with automatic help and aliases.

Debug and export

F5 shows a scrollable log with severity and channel filters. Game events and engine warnings/errors are recorded in bounded, rotating session logs. EXPORT LOGS creates a diagnostic ZIP with current and previous logs, engine output and system details.

Full-quality capture

F6 records the native game view and mixed audio to lossless RGB H.264 with floating-point PCM in an MKV. Recording runs without restarting the game. Missing FFmpeg can be downloaded, verified and privately cached on Windows x86_64; other platforms use a manual installation.

05 / THE OCTOBER CODE REVIEW

Less repeated work.
Better ownership.

The review covered active gameplay, persistence, audio, player controls, level and portal flow, interface code and the portable toolkit. Improvements were paired with regression checks.

Item tracking

Cached rigid-body handles replace repeated hierarchy searches. Spawn and grind bursts share one deferred world snapshot, while an explicit save still captures immediately. Session guards prevent stale scene restoration from repopulating a newer level.

Rendering and queries

The portal's extra viewport stops rendering when its doorway is off-screen. Placement reuses ghost geometry and physics-query setup. Room visibility checks reuse geometry and a light snapshot. Held-object prompt entries and mulcher displays update only when needed.

Sound lifecycle

Stopping pooled audio now releases its slot. Playback identifiers prevent a stale voice or movement controller from stopping a clip reused by someone else. Persistent fan and machinery sources share the Audio service while keeping local 3D attenuation. Lever and door cues follow the correct animation stages.

Safer diagnostics

Console history and output have memory limits. Developer menus share pause and input ownership. The logger records useful lifecycle events without dumping the save or every-frame state; concurrent instances have separate sessions.

MEASUREMENT / BODY DISCOVERY ONLY200 actors × 20 nested nodes × 20 lookup ticks, median of seven samples: 68,394 μs → 3,881 μs, approximately 17.6× faster. This is a focused lookup benchmark, not a claim about whole-game FPS.
What was verified?

All 38 regression scenes passed after the nested attachment correction. The suite covers physics, basket/placement, scoring, notes, imported items, progression, saves, audio ownership, startup, portal transfer, dialogue, options, developer tools, encoder and installer behaviour. Save-affecting checks used an isolated project profile. The new log viewer also received rendered and copied-addon checks.

What can a crash export tell us?

Logs flush as events occur, and a later startup preserves a previous session that did not shut down cleanly. After restarting, use F5 → EXPORT LOGS. Missing clean-exit markers can also mean forced termination; the export is diagnostic evidence, not a crash dump or a guarantee that the final event reached disk.

What remains adjustable?

Art and levels stay editable in Godot. Purchase catalogs, upgrade effects, note definitions, dialogue, sound banks, prop controls, timing, hotkeys and log limits are configurable. This remains a prototype; a test suite and targeted review cannot establish that every possible gameplay interaction is bug-free.
