# Warehouse textures

All maps are original, deterministic NumPy/Pillow artwork generated by `Tools/make_warehouse_textures.py`; no downloaded photographs or models. Metre-scale UV mapping and localized transparent decals provide concrete fissures, staggered brick courses, flaking enamel, oxidized steel, oil and damp. Native files pack their used maps; GLBs embed export-supported PBR channels.
