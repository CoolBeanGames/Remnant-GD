# Hospital texture provenance

Original deterministic NumPy/Pillow maps authored for this wing by Tools/make_hospital_textures.py. No downloaded images or models. Eleven portable PBR surfaces: clinical plaster, sage paint, cream laminate, dirty tile, worn vinyl, concrete, oxidized metal, chipped enamel, steel, rubber and aged mattress fabric. Five localized RGBA decals include damp, dust, dried blood pools, directional wall smears and faded shoe prints. Blood is environmental staining; no bodies or anatomical content. Maps are packed into native sources and embedded in standalone GLBs.
