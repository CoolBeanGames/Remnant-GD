# Rail loader and unloader stations

Two ordinary Store deliveries snap to a free end of a placed Train Rail. Rail Loader starts at 140 score; Rail Unloader starts at 170. Later purchases scale by 1.8. Use F pickup, B to show the ghost, aim at the rail end, B place, F cancel and Q retrieve. The ghost faces outward automatically on either end. An occupied station end cannot accept another station or chained rail; interior joins cannot accept stations. Carry orientation is retained on cancel, but endpoint orientation determines final placement.

Drop loose objects into the loader's open bin. Its physical walls catch them; contained bodies are queued until a car arrives at that end. Each dock scan loads up to four original items into separate available deck positions, with limited stacking. Built-on tools/scenery are included in clearance. An obstructed or full deck leaves remaining items in the bin. The world label reports LOAD / count. The 64-item cap applies to queued bodies; a captured basket remains one queue entry with its contained items nested inside it. Bodies beyond the cap remain ordinary physics in the bin.

At the unloader end, loose deck cargo is moved to the output mouth and ejected outward, one every 0.15 seconds while docked. Normal physics resumes, including a basket's contents. Built-on fan/light/plate attachments stay on the car, and players are never item cargo. This does not reverse or motorize the car: existing fan reaction, airflow, pushing and rail slopes drive it. A fan, basket or plate can receive the output to make a contraption.

## Scene and script authoring

Scripts/Actors/rail_station.gd is rail_station extending rotatable_item, UID uid://lwbr323vfqct; not an autoload and no new signals. Scenes/Actors/rail_loader.tscn and rail_unloader.tscn provide distinct native Godot prototype meshes, solid root colliders, shared reused rust/steel textures, pickup prompts and Mode LOAD/UNLOAD. Replace art independently while retaining Intake Area3D, Dock Area3D, Output Marker3D and Status Label3D. Roots use pick-up and machine-immune; placed stations join rail-station and placed-prop. Optional transfer_sound uses the pooled Audio SFX system; null is silent and safe.

Exports: mode, capacity=64, scan_interval=0.10 s, dock_tolerance=0.16 m, bin_bounds=AABB((-0.96,0.45,0.8),(1.92,1.8,1.9)), loading_batch=4, eject_speed=4.5 m/s, eject_lift=1 m/s, eject_spacing=0.15 s, transfer_sound, sound_volume=1, sound_bus=SFX. Bin bounds require the complete solid collider to fit; pickup/sensor shapes do not count. Origin is the rail endpoint; +Z points away from the track, Intake centres at (0,1.35,1.75), Output at (0,0.85,2.65). Dock centres at Z=-rail.end_margin. Matching portal/physics masks are refreshed while scanning. Tune models and bin bounds together for differently sized props.

Runtime: endpoint_end (-1/front or +1/back), weak cargo queue with relative pose/original freeze/mode/collider states, scan/ejection timers and cached world-label count. API: get_placement_pose, can_place_on, place_on, mounted_rail, release_cargo_body, get_carried_bodies, get_rail_state, restore_rail_state, portal_transform_contents. Cargo capture keeps original logical parents and identity. A basket's inner ownership stays intact during handoff rather than being released and rediscovered through stale sensors. Ordinary pickup or removal restores original physics. Carrying a station or its whole rail includes the queue and nested contents, disabling/restoring collisions through existing assembly pickup.

Rail integration: train_rail.endpoint_station(end, except=null) reserves exposed endpoints; get_carried_bodies includes mounted stations. Rail slider exposes capture_current_cargo, cargo_loading_pose(body, excluded_RIDs), accept_cargo(body, pose) and eject_cargo(body, pose, velocity). Packing checks exact collider bounds plus existing cargo and scenery; candidate search is bounded to four layers and nine positions per layer. Shared physics_items.solid_bounds ignores sensors; cargo_records and restore_basket_contents preserve saved nested actor IDs. WorldState restores all supports and queue links before normal processing, including a station purchased before its rail, then clears temporary restoring metadata.

## Save and verification

WorldState persists placement/support, station settings, queue actor IDs/body paths/local poses/original freeze state and nested basket records inside spawned_items. Dock overlap caches/timers/node handles are transient. Restoring resolves original actors after all have spawned; no duplicated cargo or reward. Moving through portals remaps original collision snapshots. Saved placed tools retain normal retrieval rules after restoration.

Tests/rail_station_test.tscn covers real ghost/confirmation, endpoint reservation, physical intake and docking, full-deck backpressure, separated loading, outward ejection, retained built light, actual basket transfer/release and save restoration with station before host rail. Existing rail/ride/carry/portal/save suites cover shared integration. Tests/rail_station_visual_test.tscn captures actual imported cargo and station/PC displays. Reports/RailStations/index.html contains reviewed engine images. These are prototype part models; no turns, powered station motor or junction routing is introduced.
