# Rail and slider

The Train Rail and Slider are loose store items. They use the existing F pickup, wheel/R rotation, B ghost / B place / F cancel and Q retrieve controls. A rail anchors to any solid surface; a slider only mounts onto a rail and its ghost snaps to the centre of the track. The slider follows the rail's local Z axis and crosses aligned, connected sections. Exposed ends retain their stops.

To extend a track, hold another rail, press B and aim at the nearest free end of an existing placed rail. The ghost aligns the new section end to end; B confirms and F cancels. Either end can be extended, and wheel rotation can reverse the new section's local direction. Sections remain independently retrievable: Q on one section removes its connections, restoring stops at the exposed neighbouring ends. A slider mounted on a removed section follows that section's ordinary pickup behavior.

Build a fan onto the slider and point its airflow along the track: the carriage travels in the opposite direction. Switch the fan off with E, rotate/rebuild it to reverse, or use two opposing fans for separate controls. A fan built onto a basket on the carriage works too. Ordinary airflow, physical pushes and gravity on a tilted rail also move the carriage. Other built props follow their support as usual.

Objects genuinely resting on the flat deck move with the carriage. Crossing its sensor in midair does not count. A resting basket brings its contained objects along, including items dropped into it later. E Empty still releases items through the basket bottom; the carriage deck catches objects directly beneath it. Pickup of a deck object releases its temporary cargo state before normal pickup rules begin. Retrieving the slider releases its deck cargo; carrying the whole rail keeps its mounted assembly together. Rail, slider, basket and fan remain immune to the grinder.

Stand on the deck to ride it. Walking remains relative to the carriage, and jumping or stepping off releases the ride normally. A flat plate or other solid supported prop can act as an additional riding surface. Player transport goes through normal character collision checks, so walls and ceilings still block movement. Transport does not add a jump upgrade; jumping requires the existing self upgrade. A player is never captured or frozen as item cargo.

## Authoring

- `Scripts/Actors/train_rail.gd` (UID `uid://bchfhr21xqpuu`): `train_rail extends rotatable_item`; `Scenes/Actors/train_rail.tscn`. Simple steel beams, seven ties, end stops. Exports `rail_length` (3 m), `carriage_height` (0.28 m), `end_margin` (0.45 m) and `connection_tolerance` (0.04 m). `refresh_geometry()` updates meshes/colliders for a changed length. API `travel_limit()`, `end_limit(end)`, `endpoint(end)`, `connected_rail(end)`, `carriage_pose(position)`, `get_placement_pose(host, hit, normal, rotation)`, static `from_collider(node)`, `get_carried_bodies()` for assembly pickup. End signs are -1/front and +1/back. Runtime connection cache and stop visibility mirror eligible neighbouring sections in group `train-rail`.
- `Scripts/Actors/rail_slider.gd` (UID `uid://0tqyb1q00n0n`): `rail_slider extends rotatable_item`; `Scenes/Actors/rail_slider.tscn`. Flat 1.8 m deck, four visual wheels, a solid deck collider and CargoArea sensor. Exports `travel_damping`, `deck_half_size`, `deck_surface`, `cargo_tolerance`. Runtime `slider_position`, `rail_basis`, cargo snapshots and scan timer. `get_placement_pose(host, hit, normal, rotation)` provides the centred ghost pose; `can_place_on(host)` accepts rail hosts; `place_on` mounts; `mounted_rail()` resolves the current section. `_integrate_forces` transfers support and rail collision exceptions when crossing an endpoint, preserving world speed, deck orientation and attached cargo; `sync_support_pose` follows moving/held rail bases. Neither script is an autoload or declares additional signals.
- `apply_drive_impulse(impulse)` projects thrust onto the track and wakes the carriage. `release_cargo_body(body)` restores original physics; `get_carried_bodies()` includes nested basket contents. `rail_cargo_owner` weak metadata lets normal pickup release a temporary capture. Captures scan at 0.1 s intervals, require a deck contact and bounded vertical speed, and preserve logical actor parents.
- `get_rail_state()` saves position, speed on the current rail's axis, relative deck basis, cargo actor IDs/body paths/local poses/original freeze state, and nested basket contents. `restore_rail_state(state, actor_resolver)` runs after saved hosts exist and resolves the existing actors. It does not create cargo copies. WorldState also persists authored rail/slider properties. Connections rebuild from the saved section transforms rather than storing node references. `portal_transform_contents` remaps cargo collision snapshots; shared teleport traversal maps nested actors and baskets once.
- `Data/Prompts/pickup_train_rail.tres`, `pickup_rail_slider.tres` and shared `carrying_rotatable.tres` provide reusable prompts. Root bodies join `pick-up` and `machine-immune`; mounted sliders also join `placed-prop` through the shared base.

The basket exposes `capture_current_contents()` for newly deposited contents on a carriage, avoids duplicate captures and excludes items still exiting its bottom. Its existing carry/release implementation remains the owner of nested basket item states.

Player movement remains `Scripts/Player/player_move_controller.gd` / Node (UID `uid://bk5h65t12flbr`), with existing player scene/body dependencies and exports. Direct deck riding uses CharacterBody3D moving-platform handling; floors frozen onto a carriage relay that carriage's velocity through normal movement. `reset_platform_motion()` clears cached transport after load, console teleport, portal crossing, respawn or wake-up. Runtime reset/last-position state is transient and does not enter saves. No player-facing controls, signals or autoloads are added. `prop_placement.get_placement_pose` providers may return an empty dictionary for ordinary anchoring, or `clearance_exclude` RIDs for touching support geometry; scenery clearance still applies.

## Verification

Script identities: train_rail.gd uid://bchfhr21xqpuu; rail_slider.gd uid://0tqyb1q00n0n. Both extend rotatable_item; neither is an autoload and neither adds signals. Basket keeps its existing script/scene and exposes capture_current_contents(). WorldState, physics_items, portal_space, player pickup/placement and shared prompt resources provide integration.

`Tests/rail_slider_test.tscn` checks real B/B placement, rail centre alignment, transverse constraint, drive impulses, fan reaction, end stops, moving bases, built attachments, actual resting cargo, loaded baskets, new basket deposits, bottom emptying, original-physics restoration and a WorldState round trip with attached fan and nested cargo. Also run the existing `manual_props_test` and `carry_attachment_test` after changing carry behavior.

`Tests/rail_chain_test.tscn` exercises joined sections, endpoint ghost placement, bidirectional transfers, reversed sections, cargo/attachments, safe disconnection and save restoration. `Tests/rail_player_test.tscn` checks the real player's platform riding, attached riding surfaces and normal dismount/teleport behavior.

Rails form straight tracks. Joins require matching endpoint positions, track direction, up direction, carriage height and physics/portal space; held or loose rails do not connect. Turns and junction routing are outside this system. Each section keeps its own anchor, so retrieving one rail does not pick up the entire track.

## Endpoint stations

Rail Loader and Rail Unloader are ordinary shop parts with end-only B/B/F ghosts, physical input/output and preserved original cargo. A station reserves its exposed rail end against chaining. Carrying a rail includes its mounted station and queued nested contents. See Data/Items/RAIL_STATIONS.md for full authoring, backpressure, saved queue and tests.
