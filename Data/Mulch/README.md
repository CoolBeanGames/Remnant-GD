# Mulcher, pellets and permanent mulch upgrades

Buy Mulcher in the computer Store for 1000 score. It installs automatically at testing_level/grinding_area/MulcherMount; no crate or placement mode. Move that marker to change its mounting position. Replace the Mesh/CollisionShape in Scenes/Mulch/mulcher_station.tscn when final art is available, retaining Output, Status and DamageGrid nodes.

Accepted grinder items add their score_object.mulch_value (clamped 1–5) times current efficiency, while the normal score/combo continues unchanged. Default values: test block 5, bottle 4, book 3, teddy bear 2, syringe 1; unconfigured items use 1. Edit the component on each item scene. Every 100 points spawns one physical pellet at Output; remainder is retained, and multiple pellets have spaced output positions. Pellets are picked up with F, thrown normally, use compact CCD physics and a larger separate aim target, and cannot be ground. Pellets only become coins after entering a Mulch Bin.

The Store delivers a portable open-top Mulch Bin for 500 score (later purchases scale by 1.8). Its non-solid Deposit sensor banks only pellets, exactly once. It remains active while carried even though held solid collisions are disabled. Baskets/ordinary items are not currencies. Banked coins are immediately persisted in the save profile; unbanked pellets and accumulated points belong to the run.

Each item adds 1 damage; default durability is 100. The machine displays a 10×10 monospaced damage grid and point/damage status. Full damage stops mulching, while the regular grinder still works. Look at the machine and press E to repair: first repair costs 100 score and takes 4 seconds; later repair costs scale by 1.6 per completed repair. Duplicate requests cannot charge again. Saved repairs resume their remaining duration; damage, points and repair count are Blackboard state.

Regular Mechanics offers Mulcher Durability (250 score, ×1.5 durability per tier) and Efficiency (500 score, ×1.25 points per item per tier), both with ×3 prices and unlimited levels. Buying both Mulcher and Mulch Bin reveals the fourth computer tab, Mulch. It uses banked coins, never ordinary score, and offers permanent item value, run speed, carry capacity, jump height/unlocking, durability, efficiency, fan power and automatic-spawner speed. Each tier doubles the corresponding bonus (spawner interval halves), stacks after regular effects, and costs ×2.5 more; tiers are unlimited. Starting costs are 5 coins except efficiency/spawner at 8. All entry/effect resources are editable in Data/Mulch and included in Data/Computer/default_catalog.tres.

Banked coins AND purchased mulch tiers survive New Game. Regular tiers, owned machinery/bins, wear, points, repair costs/timers and unbanked pellets reset. Persistent bonuses apply in the next run even before buying the machine/bin again; the shop tab unlock requires both current-run purchases. Explicit Delete Save in Options removes all run/profile data and exits.
