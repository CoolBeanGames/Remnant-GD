# World messages and handling

`Scenes/testing_level.tscn` adds **WordMessage**, **WorldGuidance** and **ceiling_chute/ChuteZone**. WordMessage is reusable anywhere with the existing Audio pool. It shows one uppercase whitespace-separated word at the centre of the screen, retains punctuation and plays the same editable Word Sound through the audio system for each word. The included `Audio/SFX/world_word.wav` is a short placeholder beep. Edit Word Seconds (0.65), Phrase Gap (1), Font Size (140), Sound Volume (0.9) and Sound Bus (SFX) on WordMessage; a blank sound is silent and safe. The label scene has no panel and automatically reduces its font size for long words.

WorldGuidance exports the four phrases and Idle Seconds (30). When the grinder is enabled but unused for 30 seconds, CHEW IT UP repeats until the first accepted item is ground. Grinding cancels both active and queued reminders immediately. Grinder player entry defers respawning to the authored concrete-room start, clears movement/look/placement/carry state and leaves score, upgrades and world items intact. DONT. DO. THAT. plays only on the first death; DO NOT LISTEN plays when the first pickup reader closes; YOU DESERVE THIS plays when the computer unlocks at 30 points. Higher-priority warnings interrupt the reminder and finish before queued lower-priority messages. All one-time histories are Flags, so restored sessions do not replay them.

Flags: `grinder used`, `message grinder death`, `message first note`, `message computer enabled`. Blackboard: `grinder_idle_seconds`, `world_messages` (live component reference), `respawn room active` (restored concrete room). The existing stage flags are preserved. Saved respawn-room state rebuilds the concrete room without re-muting SFX or resetting progress. Room removal again waits for visibility/darkness checks. The portal remains suspended until that room disappears.

The initial door requests closing once the player is fully outside the room; pending opening animation finishes before closing. `start_room.auto_close_on_exit` is adjustable and respawn copies turn it off. The portal destination now defaults to `(1600, 0, 0)`, horizontally separated at workshop floor height; layer-20 isolation and colour-correct rendering are retained.

ChuteZone covers the chute interior and exit. It gives scoring items and note bottles low friction (0.35) and damping (0.1) while inside, then duplicates their physics material and raises friction to 1 and linear/angular damping to 2 after they leave. Continuous residence for Stuck Seconds (30) removes the item and its WorldState record without score. Picking an item up releases it from cleanup and applies settled physics. Exports allow separate adjustment of chute/exit friction and damping. WorldState saves/restores the settled physics with each body's transform. Notes now default to a 2% drop chance on ceiling_chute.

Four small imported items and note bottles have the reusable `Scenes/Actors/pickup_target.tscn` Area3D with a 0.25 m adjustable radius. It only affects ray targeting; original rigid-body shapes remain unchanged. Raycaster resolves the area to its owning item, checks scenery occlusion and excludes the held/player bodies. Holding disables/restores the area with all other collision objects; portal space remaps its layer normally.

The computer's visual, screen, label and collision are uniformly restored to the source model's approximately 2.85 m height without changing its saved level placement. The fan gameplay meshes are refreshed from the corrected FBX; BladePivot uses the imported blade origin. `fan_import.gd` reapplies the supplied four-map materials to both source meshes during every reimport. Texture aliases match the FBX's missing file names so a clean import has no missing-texture errors.

## Note flow, collision audio and economy

Notes use a narrower rounded capsule, mass 0.25 kg and friction 0.02 inside the chute. ChuteZone exports note_friction, note_stall_seconds (0.75) and local note_flow_velocity (0,-1,2); a stationary note is woken and given a small velocity toward the chute exit. It still times out normally if blocked. `WorldState.cull(actor)` centralizes below-floor/chute cleanup, invokes actor.on_culled when available, untracks and deletes without score. `note_drop.on_culled` calls `Notes.release_spawned(id)` only if unread; found-note journal entries always remain excluded. Unloading a scene or collecting a note does not recycle it. Twelve bottles passing through the actual chute are covered by note_flow_economy_audio_test.

Audio exports maximum_collision_sounds=10. `Audio.play_collision(stream,position,volume=1,pitch=1)` skips new clips once ten collision-category pooled 3D players are still playing. Other sounds remain unrestricted. play_3d now returns its optional audio_player handle and both dispatch methods clear reused audio_category metadata. collision_sound_matrix and note_drop use the collision dispatcher. Finished sounds release their slots through the existing pool.

Book mass is 0.8 kg, teddy bear 0.18 kg, syringe 0.04 kg and beer bottle 0.35 kg; physical sizes and CCD remain compact. Mechanics/self upgrade curves now use multiplier 3.0, item value 3.5. Store baskets, fans, plates and auto spawners cost 1.8 times more per purchase, including upgraded fan/plate entries. Lights and the broom keep fixed prices. `computer_entry` adds escalating_store_price=false; cost(level) uses the purchase count for opted-in store entries. Progression records successful store purchases in upgrade_levels, stores independent dictionary snapshots, and the store UI shows delivered count/rising-price status. See Data/Computer/README.md for base prices.

Wake-up/room changes and dialogue authoring are documented in WAKE_UP.md and Data/Dialogue/README.md. Startup now wakes in the concrete room, while preserving the world and its progression.
