# Camera breathing

The player has **Controllers/Camera_Breathing**, a reusable camera-motion component. It combines a slow breathing wave with smooth procedural noise. The motion defaults to 15 breaths/minute, 12 mm lateral, 35 mm vertical and 6 mm forward displacement, with 6 mm noise and 0.35 degrees of gentle roll. It adds position and a small roll while keeping mouse-look pitch and yaw intact. The gameplay camera is explicitly made current. Rays, held items, ghost previews and portal views follow the camera normally. Player physics and saved player transforms remain unchanged.

Select the node in `Scenes/Player/player.tscn` or the placed player to adjust **Enabled**, **Breaths Per Minute**, **Amplitude**, **Noise Amount**, **Noise Speed**, **Roll Degrees**, **Smoothing** and **Noise Seed**. Set Enabled off to restore the authored position. With Amplitude, Noise Amount and Roll Degrees all zero there is no motion. Menus freeze the effect via Blackboard player_input_blocked and normal pause processing. Respawn resets it before applying the authored camera pose, preventing accumulated offsets.

Script: `Scripts/Player/camera_breathing.gd`; class `camera_breathing`; inheritance `Node`; UID `uid://egwe7ufljp6a`; no autoload or signals. Exported Camera references `../../Camera3D`. Defaults: enabled=true, breaths_per_minute=15, amplitude=Vector3(0.012,0.035,0.006), noise_amount=0.006, roll_degrees=0.35, noise_speed=0.25, smoothing=4, noise_seed=731. Runtime: elapsed seconds, offset Vector3, roll_offset radians and FastNoiseLite generator. Public `reset()` removes the current additive offset and resets phase. `_process(delta)` replaces only its own offset; `_exit_tree()` calls reset. Dependencies: Camera3D, Blackboard; level_sequence.respawn_player calls reset before restoring camera transform.

`Tests/camera_breathing_test.tscn` verifies bounded displacement over several breathing cycles, unchanged look pitch/yaw, menu freeze, disabled/zero-strength behavior and reset without drift. Existing portal/placement/carry and saved-position regression scenes remain applicable.
