# World interfaces and physical notes

Normal gameplay keeps its crosshair, dynamic in-world look/carry/build prompts, text-only combo, brief portal status and story/dialogue captions. Minimal traditional UI does not mean removing useful gameplay feedback. WordMessage and DialoguePlayer have show_text=true in testing_level. The actual PC screen, physical note reader, pause/options and opt-in developer tools remain. Gameplay HUD hides during PC/note sessions and returns afterward. Look text floats beside the object, carry controls beside the held model, and build controls beside the ghost. A subtle configurable 7-degree local-Y angle makes their perspective visible. See Data/GAMEPLAY_FEEDBACK.md for reusable scene authoring, optional object markers and the shared visibility group.

## PC display

E eases the gameplay camera toward an enabled PC over .55 seconds; mouse clicks and wheel scrolling address its actual screen. Mechanics/Self/Store and conditional Mulch retain their catalog/purchase rules. Esc or on-screen EXIT eases the camera back over .40 seconds, then restores camera/input/mouse and held cargo. Early Esc reverses from the current pose. Screen input waits until focus finishes; gameplay stays locked through the return. Production continues. Closed/disabled displays stop viewport rendering. Enabled flags and authored model size remain.

## Notes / controls

F collects a drop into the saved journal and raises readable paper from below the camera over .50 seconds. The world pauses. Esc lowers it over .30 seconds before returning control. Raising/putting away blends position and rotation; the camera stays still. N toggles a scrollable typed transcript, then returns to paper on a second press. N from gameplay opens the latest collected transcript; Esc returns from typing to paper, then puts paper away. Mouse wheel scrolls overflowing generated paper. Pause > Notes lists saved titles; selecting one holds its original in world. Closing returns to the paused journal after the paper lowers. Returning from the typed transcript also raises the paper. A new note cancels the previous motion safely; repeated close requests notify only once. Completed-read guidance fires when putting paper away.

## Author your physical notes

Select a note_definition in Data/Notes. Id/title/body supply the spawn pool, journal and typed transcript. Page Texture accepts your scanned/written page for the .36x.45 m sheet. Reading Scene optionally replaces it with your Node3D artwork; face +Z toward the camera, in metres. Reading Pose adjusts the art inside the holder. Use display-only geometry without score/world-item scripts. Existing world drops remain bottles until their art is changed separately.

Blank art fields print current text onto matte paper. Snapshots retain {id,title,body,definition_path,reading_scene_path,page_texture_path}. Definition paths resolve assigned art after reload; old journals fall back by catalog id or print paper. Keep art in exported res:// paths. Reading copies have no scoring, physical collision or WorldState saved duplicate.

## Clicking saved notes

pause_menu uses ItemList.item_clicked for a single left click, including the first row already selected by show_notes. item_activated retains Enter/double-click support. _click_note routes to the guarded _select_note, which rejects a closed journal or invalid row and hands control to the physical note reader once. Esc returns to the same journal. The interface regression sends actual viewport mouse events to the preselected first row and real Enter input; it does not merely call the selection method. Reports/MulchFollowup/journal contains fresh rendered checks.
